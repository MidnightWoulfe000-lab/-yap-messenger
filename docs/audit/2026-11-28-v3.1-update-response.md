## Owner decisions on 28 November 2026

- **Fallback inbox, no time limit on being away.** Every member's signed fallback inbox
  stays for as long as they are a member, and catch-up pieces go to it as well as to the
  newest current inbox. The cost (a removed member knows the old fallback keys) is stated.
- **The clock rule.** The working time never goes back and keeps ticking on the phone's
  stopwatch. A forward jump of more than 2 days asks the user before anything is expired
  or deleted. Nearby phones can only cause a "clocks disagree" note.
- **The pairing file is removed.** People who cannot meet to scan add each other through a
  group they share ("Added through <group>, not checked in person").
- **SQLCipher hides per-chat counts**, for the messages store and also the carrier store.

## Still open

- **Rollback.** A restored older copy of the whole database or its secrets, and replay of
  an older valid value, are not solved (local storage §2, open question 7). A counter in
  the Keystore would need its own review.
- **A forward jump confirmed by mistake** cannot be undone, because working time never goes
  back. Nothing in the proposals gives a recovery path.
- **The fallback inbox as a recovery route**, and whether to keep the last-resort pre-key
  for the whole membership, need review (group encryption §3.11). A pair with no usable
  session or bundle still needs a re-invite.
- **Measurements nobody has made:** the complete probe exchange at 0, 1 and 128 contacts;
  timing of storing against dropping a last copy and of a 128-label batch; carrier CPU and
  write cost under SQLCipher; the starting values for byte limits, shares, rates and
  reserves; real removal-burst capacity; battery.
- **Residual exposures, accepted for this update:** which envelopes a phone has seen, group
  membership through a re-offered last copy, and shared-label linking of attachment
  pieces (v3.1 §1.4, attachments §2).
- **One side Ready, the other cancelled** in pairing is made visible, not prevented.
- **Dependencies not yet checked:** `snow` 0.10.0 and SQLCipher through `rusqlite` on
  Android, and the `aead-stream` crate family.
- **Decoders** for images and audio run in the app process with size checks before
  decoding; they are not a sandbox, and crafted media has not been tested.
- **New vectors** for the 70-byte binding, the kind `0x02` and `0x03` outer layers, piece
  labels, and a handshake are to be written; earlier vectors covered the earlier format.
- **Earlier open items** still stand: sponsor completeness of a group log, the internet
  relay's return, the fuller simulation list, then real phones, and an independent expert
  review before any sensitive use.
