# Response to the fifth review of the v3.1 update

Answers [Codex's fifth review of the v3.1 update](2026-11-28-v3.1-update-fifth-review.md)
of 28 November 2026 (two High, nine Medium, one Low, no Critical). It follows
[the response to the fourth review](2026-11-28-v3.1-update-fourth-response.md). Revised the
same day: [group encryption](../proposals/group-encryption-v1.md) §3.2 and §3.11,
[local storage](../proposals/local-storage-v1.md), [encrypted
pairing](../proposals/encrypted-pairing.md), [rotating address
labels](../proposals/rotating-address-labels.md), [local mesh

[photos, voice notes and files](../proposals/attachments-v1.md). Choices marked "owner"
were made by the project owner on 28 November 2026. Nothing is implemented. "Fixed" means
the written proposal now says something different, not that it has been built, tested or
checked by anyone else, and every changed proposal needs another review. This is the
project's own answer, not an audit.

## Owner decisions on 28 November 2026

- **Catch-up key cost (finding 2).** The cost of keeping long-lived catch-up pre-keys is
  accepted, with the first catch-up message limited to the signed bundle and log heads,
  nothing else sent until the other phone replies, and a pinned-library test plus a
  cryptographic review still needed.
- **Replay list (finding 4).** At most 256 records per other member, never evicted. When
  one is full, further catch-ups from that member are refused with "Out of step with
  <member>: ask to be re-invited".

The earlier decisions of the same day are listed in
[the previous response](2026-11-28-v3.1-update-response.md) and
[the fourth response](2026-11-28-v3.1-update-fourth-response.md).

## Still open

- **Direct transfer channel** and the `FILE_OFFER` / `FILE_CHUNK` / `FILE_ACK` /
  `FILE_DONE` encodings, the initiator message carrying the week, canonical PSK-info and
  prologue bytes and bounded dispatch are still not written.
- **Dependencies not yet checked on Android:** `snow` 0.10.0, SQLCipher through
  `rusqlite` and the `aead-stream` crate family.
- **Catch-up historical exposure:** the pinned-library experiment and the separate
  cryptographic review (finding 2). Catch-up recovery is unproven until its tests pass.
- **Rollback** of a restored older copy of the stores, and a wrongly confirmed forward
  clock jump, remain unsolved (local storage §8, open question 7).
- **Unmeasured starting values:** the storage tiers, the 64 MB reserve, the work-gate
  and ordinary rates, the waiting-line times and offer limits, and the 256-record cap.
  Also probe timing, carrier cost under SQLCipher and real removal-burst capacity.
- **Group-state shortcut:** its comparison with full derivation is still missing, so its
  performance claim cannot be used.
- **Earlier open items** still stand: sponsor completeness of a group log, the internet
  relay's return, real phones, and an independent expert review before any sensitive use.
