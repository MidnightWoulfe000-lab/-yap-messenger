of 28 November 2026 (four High, seven Medium, one Low, no Critical). It follows
[the response to the previous review](2026-11-28-v3.1-update-response.md). Revised the same

checked by anyone else, and every changed proposal needs another review. This is the

| 1 | High | Accepted, partly answered | The address guarantee and the recovery guarantee are now stated separately. The fallback inbox always reaches a member; whether the message can then be opened is the job of a **catch-up bundle**: the bundle in the signed `JoinRequest` keeps its signed pre-key and post-quantum last-resort pre-key for as long as the member is in the group, is used only for catch-up set-ups and is never replaced by the monthly change (owner). So the stale-bundle exchange is specified in four steps: R cannot read S's set-up and treats it only as a hint; R opens a fresh session to S's catch-up bundle and sends S, at S's fallback inbox, its current signed bundle and log heads; S can always read it and the two resynchronise the missing log and keys over that session (§3.7); and the same the other way round. Because the failed set-up proves nothing about who sent it, a phone sends at most one catch-up answer per claimed sender per hour and at most 10 an hour in total, answers only to the claimed sender's own catch-up bundle and inbox (so a faker gets nothing readable), and records base keys used with catch-up pre-keys for the whole membership so a replayed set-up is refused. If one side has been removed or lost its catch-up keys in a reset, the app shows "Out of step with <member>: ask to be re-invited". "However long ago" now applies to the address, and "no time limit on being away" to members who both stay members. The privacy cost is narrower: a removed member who knows the fallback inboxes can recognise catch-up traffic from S to R hour after hour and see its size, it is not unlinkable to them, and the possession and re-offering observations in v3.1 §1.4 still apply. The claim that they cannot "follow anyone" is gone. The cost of keeping catch-up pre-keys is stated: someone who later takes the whole phone and recorded earlier set-ups could open them, adding little since they already hold the live sessions. ## Owner decisions on 28 November 2026

- **The catch-up bundle.** Each member's signed join bundle keeps its signed pre-key and
  post-quantum last-resort pre-key for as long as that member is in the group, is used
  only for catch-up set-ups, and is never replaced by the monthly bundle change. With the
  fallback inbox it lets two members who both stay in the group always reach and open each
  other's catch-up pieces. The cost for a later full phone capture is stated.

The earlier decisions of the same day (the fallback inbox with no time limit on being
away, the clock rule, the pairing file removed in favour of adding friends through a group,
and SQLCipher for both stores) are listed in
[the previous response](2026-11-28-v3.1-update-response.md).

## Still open

- **Recovery without a route.** If one of two members has been removed, or a phone lost its
  catch-up keys in a reset, end-to-end catch-up does not work and a re-invite is needed.
  The long-lived catch-up pre-keys are an owner decision and still need review.
- **Rollback** of a restored older copy of the whole store, and a wrongly confirmed forward
  clock jump, remain unsolved (local storage §8, open question 7).
- **Missing tests:** reserve exhaustion, failed commits, long readers and disk used by
  another app (local storage); a copied label in an invalid batch, a valid outer layer
  with invalid inner content, and a collision candidate that opens only on a later key
  (group encryption §3.11); a deliberate whole-file integrity check for SQLCipher.
- **Direct transfer encodings** and the frame work bounds (see above) are not yet written.
- **Measurements nobody has made:** probe timing at 0, 1 and 128 contacts, store versus
  drop timing, carrier cost under SQLCipher, the floor, headroom and reserve amounts, and
  real removal-burst capacity. The size figures have not been reproduced by anyone but the
  project, and the replay and restart test is still to be written.
- **Pairing:** a Ready responder whose partner cancelled is made visible, not prevented.
- **Dependencies not yet checked:** `snow` 0.10.0, SQLCipher through `rusqlite` and the
  `aead-stream` crate family on Android.
- **Earlier open items** still stand: sponsor completeness of a group log, the internet
  relay's return, the fuller simulation list, then real phones, and an independent expert
  review before any sensitive use.
